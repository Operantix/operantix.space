About this app

Dialer Vault is the world's most sophisticated stealth privacy vault camouflaged inside an authentic, fully operational native Android phone dialer. Hide sensitive photos, private videos, secret audio recordings, and classified documents behind an innocent dial keypad.

📸 Intruder Break-In Selfie

If someone enters the wrong PIN 3 times, the app silently snaps their photo using the front camera with zero shutter sound or flash, logging date, time, and wrong PIN.

🔄 Flip-to-Lock & Shake Panic

Turn your phone face-down on a table or give it a quick shake—the vault instantly shuts down and reverts to the standard dialer screen with haptic feedback.

🕵️ Recent Apps Blur (FLAG_SECURE)

Screenshots are blocked inside the vault, and opening Android's recent apps switcher obscures the vault thumbnail so bystanders can't see your private media.

📴 Auto-Lock on App Switch

Leaving the vault to home or multitasking automatically locks the session, returning back to the dialer screen so you never leave your secrets open.

🔒 AES-256 Military Encryption

Files imported into Dialer Vault are encrypted at byte level. Original files are permanently deleted from Android gallery to prevent forensic recovery.

📞 Genuine Phone Calling

Make phone calls, search contacts, view missed/incoming calls, and refresh history. To anyone looking, it's just your standard default phone dialer app.
